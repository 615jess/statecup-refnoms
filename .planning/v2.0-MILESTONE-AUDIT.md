| adminemail.gs (system) | S (Sent), T (SentAt) | Shared S — idempotency guard |

Status column S is written by 3 phases but with sequential, non-overlapping state transitions: Not Sent (Phase 2) → Sent (Phase 4) → Confirmed (Phase 3). Phase 4 idempotency guard prevents overwriting Confirmed back to Sent.
